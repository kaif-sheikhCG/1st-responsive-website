THE STUDIO

We print like it's still 1987 and we're kind of proud of it.

Overprint has been running risograph presses since 2016 — machines that print one flat color at a time, stencil by stencil, with soy-based ink that never quite lines up perfectly. We think that's the best part. Every job comes back a little different from the last, which is exactly what you want when you're trying not to look like everyone else's PDF.

  • Every ink mixed in-house, by hand
  • Same job, run by the same two hands start to finish
  • Proofs back within 48 hours
  • We'll tell you honestly if your file won't print well

HOW A JOB RUNS

Four passes, one sheet at a time

Every job moves through the shop in the same order — it's the only way the colors line up.

01 / Design

Design & files

Send finished art or work with our in-house designer. We check separations before anything touches the press.

02 / Separate

Color separation

Your file is split into individual layers, one per ink color, each becoming its own stencil.

03 / Print

Print, layer by layer

Each color runs through the press by hand. Sheets dry between passes so inks don't smear or bleed.

04 / Finish

Dry, trim & ship

Full drying overnight, trim to size, then boxed up for shipping or ready for pickup.
