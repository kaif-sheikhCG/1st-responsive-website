THE GYM

Eighteen feet of wall, reset every Monday.

Crux opened in 2018 with four walls and a folding table for a front desk. We've grown into a full facility, but the philosophy hasn't changed: routes should get reset often enough that regulars never get bored, and beginners should never feel like they wandered into someone else's gym.

  • Routes reset every week across all four grades
  • Free beginner orientation with every first visit
  • Coaches on the floor during all open hours
  • Gear rental included, no membership required

YOUR FIRST SESSION

How to send your first route

Every new climber goes through the same four steps — it's the fastest way to get comfortable on the wall.

01

Chalk up

A little chalk on dry hands. Too much and you'll slide right off — we'll show you the right amount.

02

Read the route

Look up before you climb. Trace the holds with your eyes and plan three moves ahead.

03

Climb

Start on yellow tape. Move with your legs, not your arms — they'll last a lot longer.

04

Fall safely

Bend your knees, land flat-footed, roll if you need to. Falling is half the sport.
